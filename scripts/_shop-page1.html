Make sure you read this

A few house rules so your first rental goes smoothly.

  1. 01
    Do first-time clients get delivery? No. First-time clients pick up in person. Delivery may be available for returning clients.
  2. 02
    What ID is required? A valid Ghana Card is required at pickup (Bomso, near Obesse Gaming Center, Kumasi, Ghana).
  3. 03
    How do I pay? Pay online with Paystack (card / mobile money), or choose pay on pickup (cash) where offered.
  4. 04
    Can someone else pick up for me? The account holder should be present with valid ID at pickup.
  5. 05
    Can I cancel? Yes. Cancel up to 48 hours before pickup for a full refund (minus processing fees).
